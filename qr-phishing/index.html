Training Demo — QR Phishing (Quishing)

This is a QR phishing ("quishing") demo. The QR code above encodes a phishing URL. Here's why it's dangerous:

  • Bypasses email filters — filters scan text links, not images
  • No hover preview — you can't hover a QR code to see where it goes
  • Mobile = fewer defenses — phones lack corporate web filtering
  • Looks legitimate — can be printed on fake invoices, posters, desk drops
  • Fix: Use a QR scanner that shows URL before opening, then check domain carefully
