Travelling on a budget doesn't mean sacrificing experience. With the right strategies, you can explore incredible destinations without emptying your savings account. Here are ten tips seasoned travellers swear by.

1. Book flights on Tuesdays — airlines reportedly release discounted seats midweek when demand drops. Use incognito mode to avoid dynamic pricing cookies.

2. Stay in local guesthouses — skip chain hotels and use local booking platforms. You'll pay a fraction of the price and get a more authentic experience.
