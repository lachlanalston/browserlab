That site was designed to manipulate you.

Every element — the popup, the countdown, the cookie banner, the ads — was engineered to get you to click something you didn't intend to. This is a training demo.

Dark patterns you saw on that page

  • Prize popup — "1,000,000th visitor" prizes are always fake. They either install malware, steal your details, or charge a "shipping fee" on a product that never arrives
  • Countdown timer — the timer resets every page load. The urgency is completely manufactured
  • Fake virus warning in an ad — browsers cannot detect viruses. Any ad claiming your PC is infected is lying to get you to install something
  • Misleading cookie consent — "Accept All" is large and blue; "Reject" is tiny, grey, and uses guilt language ("limited experience"). This is an illegal dark pattern in the EU
  • Accepting cookies triggered another popup — rewarding consent with more intrusion is a deliberate dark pattern
  • Clickbait recommended content — "Doctors HATE this" and "This $9 trick" are scam signals. Clicking leads to fake health products, investment scams, or malware downloads

Why "your PC has a virus" ads are always fake

  • A webpage has no access to your filesystem — it cannot scan for viruses
  • Real antivirus alerts appear as system notifications or inside the antivirus app — never in a browser tab
  • Clicking these ads installs fake "cleaners" that are often the actual malware
  • Legitimate security software never advertises through web banners

Protecting yourself on ad-heavy sites

  • Install a browser ad blocker (uBlock Origin is free and effective) — blocks most malicious ads before they load
  • Never click popup prizes, countdown offers, or "you've been selected" banners
  • On cookie banners — look for "Reject All" or "Manage Preferences"; don't click "Accept All" by default
  • If an ad claims your PC is infected — close the tab, do not click anything inside it
  • Treat "Recommended for you" content sections as ads — they are paid placements, not editorial recommendations
