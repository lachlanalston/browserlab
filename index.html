Understanding the Adversary: Educational Simulations

Our library provides MSP clients with hands-on exposure to modern cyber threats through controlled, simulated environments. We do not host live attacks; rather, we provide meticulously constructed and entirely synthetic examples of real-world threat vectors—including phishing campaign layouts, credential harvesting page structures, and malware components. This dedicated resource allows your team to review the anatomy of an attack in a safe, non-live setting. By studying these demonstrations (hosted on GitHub), client teams can enhance their detection capabilities and improve readiness without any risk to live systems or data.
